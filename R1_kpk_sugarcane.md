Compiled 2026-10-03. KP sugarcane belt: Mardan, Charsadda, Peshawar, Nowshera, Swabi.
Sugarcane is a long-duration crop (~8–10 month growing season in KP), with a **short
KP season and frost risk** that make variety and timing choices sharper.

---

## Right now (early October)
KP sugarcane **autumn planting is September–October**
([SCRI Mardan](https://agrires.kp.gov.pk/page/sugar_crops_research_institute_mardan_khyber_pakhtunkhwa);
[sugarcane farming](https://www.timesofagriculture.pk/sugarcane-farming-in-pakistan-2025/)).
So in early October a KP grower is **planting the autumn crop** (and tending a standing
crop). A pilot now should centre on **disease-free seed-cane (setts), timely autumn
planting, and red-rot avoidance**, plus borer/pyrilla scouting on standing cane.

## C. Diseases
| Local/English | Field signs | Official response (verify) | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|
| **لال سڑن Red rot** (the killer) | top 3rd/4th leaves yellow & dry; split stalk shows **reddened internodes with white patches** and a sour smell | **no cure** — grow **resistant varieties**, use **disease-free setts**, **remove & destroy affected clumps**, crop rotation, good drainage/airflow | **high** |
| چابک کنڈوا Whip smut | black whip-like structure from the top | resistant varieties; remove & destroy affected shoots | low |
| رتون سٹنٹنگ Ratoon stunting | stunted ratoon, thin stalks | disease-free setts; hot-water sett treatment; don't ratoon diseased fields | low |

**Red rot is to KP sugarcane what leaf curl virus is to cotton and yellow rust to
wheat** — the headline disease, managed by prevention (resistant variety + clean
setts), not cure. Recent SCRI Mardan varieties are bred for red-rot resistance.
*(confidence: high.)*

## D. Weather / other
- **Frost (KP valleys):** timely planting and earthing-up help; a real KP constraint (SCRI).
- **Waterlogging:** raises top-borer and red-rot incidence — keep drainage (mgmt).
- **Lodging:** prop/tie tall cane to prevent lodging in wind (mgmt).
- **Harvest:** harvest at full maturity for sugar; strip leaves; **deliver to the mill within 24–48 h** (sucrose drops after cutting) ([sugarcane farming](https://www.timesofagriculture.pk/sugarcane-farming-in-pakistan-2025/)).
- **Ratoon:** don't ratoon heavily pest/disease-infested fields.

## E. Safety
Same as other crops: PPE, not into wind, keep others/animals away, wash after,
labelled storage, pre-harvest interval, poisoning first aid. **No doses invented.**

## 5 facts worth quoting
1. KP sugarcane is **autumn-planted Sept–Oct** (SCRI Mardan).
2. **Red rot is the headline disease; there is no cure** — manage with resistant varieties + disease-free setts (SCRI/PARC).
3. **Disease-free 3-budded setts at ~90 cm rows** are the recommended establishment (SCRI).
4. **Earthing-up (May–Jun) stops the Gurdaspur borer** emerging, and destroying trash cuts the borer complex (PARC/adv-tech).
5. **Deliver cut cane to the mill within 24–48 h** — sucrose falls after cutting (sugarcane farming).

## 3 things NOT to claim
1. No insecticide dose until an official Pakistani source is cited and an agronomist approves it.
2. Don't call a variety "red-rot-proof" — resistance erodes; confirm the current SCRI Mardan list.
3. Don't ship Pashto text as checked — Urdu/Roman only until a native speaker verifies Pashto.
