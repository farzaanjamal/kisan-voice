## Right now (early October)
Sindh rice is **kharif** and Sindh harvests **earlier than Punjab**: IRRI-6 harvest
**begins in October** in Sindh ([harvest guide](https://pakistanrice.com/harvest-calendar/);
[importer guide](https://teftglobal.com/pakistan-rice-harvest-season/)). So in early
October a Sindh rice farmer is at **grain-fill to early harvest**: the live topics are
**harvest timing, drying and clean threshing**, plus a late watch for **brown
planthopper and blast**. A 7-day pilot now should centre on those, not sowing.

## B. Key seasonal decisions

| Decision | Practice (verify) | Timing | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Variety | blast/BLB-tolerant approved variety (IRRI-6, Sada Hayat, etc.) | nursery | medium — get RRI-Dokri list |
| Nursery age | transplant at 25–35 days; aged nursery cuts yield | transplant | high |
| **Water (AWD)** | alternate wetting & drying; **don't let water stagnate** — stagnation favours BPH | whole season | **high** |
| Nitrogen | apply in 2–3 splits (basal, ~35 DAT, before flowering); **avoid excess N** (worsens BPH, blast, BLB) | per schedule | **high** |
| Scouting | weekly; spray only on ETL | tillering → grain | high |
| Stubble | plough in / destroy straw & stubble after harvest (kills stem-borer larvae, cuts BLB) | post-harvest | high |
| Harvest timing | at 85–95% straw-coloured panicles; dry 2–3 days | Oct–Nov | high |

The single strongest, safest lever across Sindh rice is **water + nitrogen
management**: alternate wetting-and-drying and avoiding excess nitrogen reduce brown
planthopper, blast and bacterial leaf blight together — all well sourced, all
non-chemical. *(confidence: high.)*

## E. Safety
Same as cotton: PPE when spraying, never against the wind, keep others/animals away,
wash after, store pesticide in its labelled container away from food/children, observe
the label's pre-harvest interval, know poisoning signs and seek help. **No doses or
first-aid specifics invented — take them from the label and an official source.**

## 5 facts worth quoting
1. Sindh harvests rice **earlier than Punjab**; IRRI-6 harvest **begins in October** (harvest guides).
2. **Alternate wetting-and-drying + avoiding excess nitrogen** cut BPH, blast and BLB together — non-chemical (IPM package; Pakistan mgmt).
3. **Destroying stubble after harvest** kills overwintering stem-borer larvae — cheap and effective for small farmers (Pakistan review).
4. BPH ETL ≈ **10–15 hoppers/hill**; foliar blast ≈ **3–5 lesions/leaf** (IPM package).
5. Harvest at **85–95% straw-coloured panicles**, then dry **2–3 days** before threshing (PARC).
