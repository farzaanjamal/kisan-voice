## Right now (early October) — the timely moment
Wheat sowing in KP runs **~25 October–15 November** (irrigated 1–25 Nov; rainfed
25 Oct–20 Nov; Bannu 25 Oct–25 Nov) ([KP Wheat Production Technology](https://zarat.kp.gov.pk/assets/uploads/publications/Wheat%20Production%20Technology.pdf);
[ARI Tarnab study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/280722592)). So in early
October a KP farmer is at **land preparation and about to sow** — the single most
decision-rich moment of the season. A pilot now should centre on **variety choice
(rust-resistant!), seed rate, seed treatment, and timely sowing** — not mid-season
pests. This is the best-timed of your three crops for a pilot.

**Timely sowing is the headline message:** each day's delay in sowing after ~20
November reduces grain yield (≈36 kg/ha/day reported in Punjab), and optimal KP yields
come from late-Oct–mid-Nov sowing ([ARI Tarnab / sowing-date study](https://www.academia.edu/91700676)). *(confidence: high.)*

## B. Key seasonal decisions

| Decision | Practice (verify) | Timing | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Variety** | choose a **yellow-rust-resistant approved variety** (recent cultivars e.g. Pirsabak-2015, Pakistan-2013 resist better; older lines have lost resistance) | at sowing | **high** |
| **Sowing date** | sow on time (25 Oct–15 Nov KP); late sowing cuts yield sharply | now | **high** |
| Seed rate | ~50 kg/acre drill (timely); 55–60 kg late; 20–25% more for broadcast/zero-till | sowing | high |
| Seed treatment | treat seed against smut/bunt (and termite in rainfed) | sowing | medium |
| Sowing method | drill sowing beats broadcasting (even stand, less seed) | sowing | high |
| Fertiliser | DAP (phosphorus) at sowing; nitrogen in splits; avoid excess N (worsens rust) | per schedule | medium–high |
| Irrigation | no water shortage at tillering, booting, grain filling | Dec–Mar | high |
| Weeds | control early (wild oat/Phalaris, broadleaf weeds compete heavily) | tillering | high |

## E. Safety
Same as cotton/rice: PPE when spraying, never against the wind, keep others/animals
away, wash after, store pesticide in its labelled container away from food/children,
observe the label's pre-harvest interval, know poisoning signs and seek help. **No
doses or first-aid specifics invented.**

## F. Conflicts / notes
1. Seed rate per acre varies by source (~45–55 kg drill, higher late/broadcast) — use the KP leaflet's figure for the exact method.
2. "Sowing before 20 Nov" (PARC, national) vs "25 Oct–15 Nov" (KP leaflet) — both agree on *timely, by mid–late November*; use the KP window.
3. Variety resistance is **not durable** — a variety resistant today can succumb as rust races shift; always confirm the *current* recommended list with ARI Tarnab.

## 5 facts worth quoting
1. KP wheat is sown **~25 Oct–15 Nov**; **late sowing cuts yield sharply** (KP leaflet; ARI Tarnab).
2. **Yellow (stripe) rust is the headline wheat disease in Pakistan**, and a **resistant variety is the most economical control** (Pakistan overview; recent-cultivar study).
3. Most of Pakistan's **recent** wheat cultivars have **moderate** rust resistance, while many **historic** lines have lost it — so variety choice must be current (Frontiers 2024).
4. Keep **no water shortage at tillering, booting and grain-filling** (PARC).
5. **Treated/certified seed** prevents loose smut; **drill sowing** beats broadcasting (KP leaflet).

## 3 things NOT to claim
1. No fungicide/pesticide dose until an official Pakistani source is cited and an agronomist approves it.
2. Don't call any variety "rust-proof" — resistance shifts with rust races; confirm the current list.
3. Don't ship Pashto text as checked — the draft is Urdu/Roman only until a native speaker verifies Pashto.
