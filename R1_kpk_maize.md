## Right now (early October)
KP autumn/kharif maize is sown ~June–August and **harvested October–November**
([maize seasons](https://saremco.com.pk/maize-crop-season-in-pakistan-a-complete-guide/)).
So in early October a KP maize farmer is at **grain-fill to harvest** — the live
topics are **harvest timing, drying and storage (maize weevil)**, with a late watch
for **fall armyworm**. A pilot now should centre on those.

## 5 facts worth quoting
1. KP autumn maize is **harvested Oct–Nov**; KP maize is mostly a **smallholder OPV** crop (KP leaflet; seasons).
2. **Fall armyworm is now the most damaging maize pest in Pakistan** (FAW study; KP leaflet).
3. **Hand-collecting and destroying FAW egg masses, plus avoiding staggered sowing**, are front-line non-chemical controls (KP leaflet).
4. **Maize stem borer can cut yield 10–30%**; destroying stubble and deep ploughing break its cycle (maize mgmt; KP leaflet).
5. **Avoid water stress at silking** — it's the most critical stage for kernel set (agronomy).

## 3 things NOT to claim
1. No insecticide dose until an official Pakistani source is cited and an agronomist approves it.
2. Don't present one variety as "FAW-proof" — manage FAW by scouting + culture, not by variety alone.
3. Don't ship Pashto text as checked — Urdu/Roman only until a native speaker verifies Pashto.
